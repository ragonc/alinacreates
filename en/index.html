About me

Curiosity drives me, especially when an idea turns into something that reaches people. As a hairdresser I learnt to listen well and to find out what someone really needs. At Impact Hub Zürich I saw how single posts turn into a campaign for an entire event. Today at TINGS I develop social media concepts, plan shoots and edit videos, and for some formats I appear on camera myself. I most enjoy seeing a project through from the first idea to the finished post, because it lets me think creatively and try out straight away what works.
